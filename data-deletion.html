Deleting your data

The Aeroport AI app (developer: Javohir Qurbonaliyev) stores user data (name, phone, flight reminders, settings, search history) only on your device. It is not permanently stored on a server.

How to delete

  1. In the app: open the Profile or Settings section and delete your data (profile, reminders, search history).
  2. Full deletion: uninstalling the app from your device permanently erases ALL data associated with it.
  3. Request: if you need further help, email us below — we will review your request within 7 business days.

What data is deleted

Profile (name, phone), flight reminders, settings (language, theme), search history — all erased from the device. Since this data is not stored on a server, it disappears completely when the app is uninstalled.
